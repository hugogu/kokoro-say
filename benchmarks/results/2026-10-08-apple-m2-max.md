The machine was in ordinary desktop use during the run: the one-minute load average was 6.5 at the
start and 6.7 at the end, on 12 cores. `ksay` is the only engine that spreads over many cores,
so its times are, if anything, pessimistic. Three complete runs on this day gave 1.54 to 1.71 s,
4.45 to 4.82 s and 14.9 to 16.5 s for `ksay` on the short, medium and long text; this report is the last one.

Wall time of one command from launch until its file is written, median of six runs (three for the
long text) after a warm-up run. Memory is the command's peak resident set size. For `say` that is
the `say` process; the system speech services it calls were each under 20 MB when sampled.

Whisper transcribes the recording; word error rate is computed after both texts go through Whisper's
English text normalizer. For the numbers set, formatting between digits is ignored too, so `415-555-0132`
matches `415 555 0132`.
